  - **Mobile LCP Preloading:** Added layout-level preloads for `cloud_only_mini.webp` and `cloud_sun_mini.webp` in [layout.tsx](file:///C:/Users/User/Desktop/agenc-ia/apps/getxobelaeskola-web/src/app/[locale]/layout.tsx) and set `fetchPriority="high"` inside [Navbar.tsx](file:///C:/Users/User/Desktop/agenc-ia/apps/getxobelaeskola-web/src/components/layout/Navbar.tsx) to ensure header clouds (which the browser identifies as mobile LCP targets due to responsive sizing) load at 0ms.
